How CampusLoop Works

Simple, campus-verified sharing designed around student life and deadlines.

1

List or Search

Snap a photo of textbooks, cables, or project gear you are not using, or query what you urgently need right now.

2

Connect & Verify

Every user is authenticated via their official university email (.edu). Chat safely and set up a spot on campus.

3

Loop & Return

Meet at designated Safe Zones (like the library desk), use the resource, and loop it back once the semester wraps.
